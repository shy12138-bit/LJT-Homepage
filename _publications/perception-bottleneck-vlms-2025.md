## On the Perception Bottleneck of VLMs for Chart Understanding

**First author:** Junteng Liu

**Co-authors:** Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, Junxian He

**Venue:** Arxiv (2025)

This paper investigates the perception bottleneck of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) for chart understanding. It is Junteng Liu's first-author publication (2025). The work has a GitHub code repository available: **Vision4Chart**.

**Keywords:** VLM, Hallucination in VLMs, Chart Understanding, Vision-Language Models
