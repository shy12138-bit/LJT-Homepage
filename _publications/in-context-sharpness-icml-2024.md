## In-Context Sharpness as Alerts: An Inner Representation Perspective for Hallucination Mitigation

**Authors:** Shiqi Chen, Miao Xiong, Junteng Liu, Zhengxuan Wu, Teng Xiao, Siyang Gao, Junxian He

**Venue:** ICML 2024 (International Conference on Machine Learning)

This paper takes an inner representation perspective for hallucination mitigation, proposing in-context sharpness as alerts for detecting and mitigating hallucinations in large language models. Junteng Liu is a co-author of this paper, published at ICML 2024.

**Keywords:** Hallucination Mitigation, Inner Representation, In-Context Learning, ICML
