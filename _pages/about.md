## About Me

**Junteng Liu** is a first-year PhD candidate at the **HKUST NLP Group** (Hong Kong University of Science and Technology), under the supervision of **Professor Junxian He**. He completed his B.Eng. (2020-2024) at **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)**, graduating in June 2024, and is now pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science (2024-Present) at HKUST.

His research focuses on **natural language processing and machine learning**, with specific interests in:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM truthfulness and Interpretability**

He has received the **Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship** at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and has gained research experience as an intern at **MINIMAX** (February 2025 - Present), **Tencent WXG** (June 2024 - September 2024), and **Shanghai AI Lab** (June 2023 - December 2023).
